## Limitations

- Module-global reads are complete for the non-constant globals in module_globals.csv (a targeted re-run of the globals_scan.py classifier over runtime routines), not the 5-entry read_by_sample. PARAMETER constants and the MESHES container (whose components are the mesh_field rows) are excluded. Globals that no mesh kernel touches are not in module_globals.csv and therefore not in this matrix.
- Mesh-field access comes from routine_field_access.csv. Pointer-alias and argument effects follow that file's rules; arg_unknown globals are counted as RW. Writes that routine_field_access.csv attributes to IBAR/JBAR/KBAR through the RESIDUAL alias (pres.f90:5918) are treated as reads.
- The call graph is static and flow-insensitive; runtime IF guards (for example CC_IBM at main.f90:786) are ignored, so stage membership is an over-approximation. Prev-step reads use main-loop call-site order, not statement order inside a call tree.
- carried_between_steps (rw_matrix_fields.csv) is a lower bound: a field whose first reader in the loop also passes it INTENT(INOUT) counts as written at that call site, which is why U is not marked (COMPUTE_VISCOSITY passes it to CUTFACE_VELOCITIES, INTENT(INOUT) at ccib.f90:750).
- Stage names for main-loop call sites are a manual classification (ROOT_STAGE in rw_matrix.py); HVAC, CREATE_OR_REMOVE_OBSTRUCTIONS, STOP_CHECK and CHECK_STABILITY are other/shared.
