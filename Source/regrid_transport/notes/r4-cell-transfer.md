# R4: conservative limited prolongation and the clip (CellTransfer)

Files: `CellTransfer.H/.cpp`, `tests/test_celltransfer.cpp` (ctest `regrid_transport_celltransfer`, `regrid_transport_celltransfer_np4`). Restriction is the existing `average_down_cells` (`LevelOps.H`).

**What it does.** `prolong_conserved(fine, crse, ...)` fills a new fine patch from the coarse level for the conserved densities rho*Z_n of all tracked species (components of one MultiFab). Per parent (the coarse cell) and component: monotonised-central limited slope per refined direction, zero at a local extremum; children = parent + slopes times the child offset (offsets symmetric, so the children average to the parent); one factor alpha scales all slopes of the parent so that every child stays inside [max(min of the 3x3x3 neighbourhood, 0), max of the neighbourhood]. rho is the caller's sum over species, so Y_n = rho*Z_n/rho is in [0,1] and sums to 1 whenever all rho*Z_n >= 0. Old fine data (if given) is copied bitwise over the overlap afterwards, so only cells without fine data are interpolated.

**Clip (ruling: conservative, counted).** If a child is still below the floor (a parent below the floor itself, or round-off), the children are raised to the floor and the others scaled so that the children's sum equals r^3 times the parent again; a parent below the floor gets piecewise-constant children (sum kept, still negative) and is also counted as `unfixable`. `ProlongStats.clips` counts every such parent-component pair (FR-025); the positive-data tests require 0.

**Tests (all pass at 1, 2, 4 ranks, 42 checks per rank).**
- Regrid scenario (old patch, new patch with partial overlap: one part dropped, one new), ratios 2, 4, (2,1,2), (4,1,4), smooth and rough positive data (40x fronts, many limited parents): composite integral of every species (uncovered coarse plus fine, long double sums) changes by less than 1e-12 relative; measured: below 1e-16 in all six cases. Overlap with old fine data bitwise equal. Zero clips. Y in [0,1] and summing to 1 to 1e-14.
- Operator alone, rough data, ratios 2 and 4: every parent's children average to the parent; every child inside the 3x3x3 min/max of the parent (no new extremes); linear field reproduced at the child centres to 1e-13 for ratio (4,2,2); constant field bitwise; result bitwise independent of the box split of the fine level.
- Negative controls: one fine cell changed by 1e-3 relative is detected by the budget (change 1e-10 to 1e-8, over the 1e-12 limit); unlimited central slopes would give negative values at many parents of the rough field (the limiter matters); a negative parent is counted as a clip and as unfixable; `use_floor = false` counts nothing; `clip_children` unit cases (raise and rescale keeps the sum).

**Not verified / limits.** The coarse ghost layer must be filled by the caller (same-level exchange and physical boundary) before the call; non-periodic domain edges rely on that. Host loops only (regrid-time host work is allowed until Phase 11, NFR-043); a device version is not written. rho is not interpolated on its own: it must be rebuilt as the species sum (FR-025 realizability hook with Role 4). Temperature and other derived fields are recomputed by the kernels, not here. Behaviour at a physical wall or next to OBST cells is not covered (Phase 5).
